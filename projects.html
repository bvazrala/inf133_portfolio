Briefly

IoT AI briefing station

An ESP32 device that reads you the morning without handing you a phone. I'm leading a team of two.

Why it matters

  • The alarm keeps its own time, so a dead router can't make you sleep through it.
  • Summaries are generated on your own network, so nothing about your morning leaves the house.

What I did

  • Wrote C++ firmware around FreeRTOS tasks so the alarm keeps its own time when Wi-Fi drops.
  • Built a Python MQTT gateway that summarises feeds with a local LLM, keeping personal data on the home network.
  • Streamed device telemetry to Azure IoT Hub to chart usage patterns on a live dashboard.

Yield

What safety costs in speed

Training a family of driving agents that differ only in how much they are penalised for crashing, then measuring what each one trades away.

Why it matters

  • Manufacturers decide how assertive a self-driving car should be, and that decision ends up inside a reward function.
  • Sweeping the collision penalty puts a number on what caution costs: from the most aggressive setting to the most cautious, collisions fell 79% and average speed fell 25%.
  • Transfer to merges and roundabouts tests whether that tuning survives a road the agent never trained on.

What I did

  • Trained DQN agents in highway-env, where surrounding traffic follows the Intelligent Driver and MOBIL models.
  • Held hyperparameters and the step budget fixed so the reward specification is the only variable.
  • Tested zero-shot transfer to merge and roundabout scenarios the agents never trained on.
When
Summer 2026
Made for
CS 175, Project in AI
Team
Three people
Built with
Python, PyTorch, Stable-Baselines3, Gymnasium, highway-env

Big Five graphical models

Structure learning over personality data

Fifty survey questions go in. Five personality traits come out without the model being told what to look for.

Why it matters

  • The model was never told the five traits existed. Community detection recovered all five, scoring a perfect 1.00 against the known labels.
  • A question's neighbours in the learned graph predict its answer, lifting accuracy from 64.5% to 77.6%.

What I did

  • Fit a pairwise Ising model with L1 regularisation over 50 items from the openpsychometrics Big Five dataset.
  • Ran community detection on the learned graph, recovering all five traits exactly.
  • Predicted masked responses from each question's graph neighbourhood, beating the baseline by 13 points.

ASL computer control

Typing and media control from hand signs

Type and drive media playback with hand signs alone. No keyboard, no mouse.

Why it matters

  • 97% typing accuracy in testing, which is the line between something usable and a demo.

What I did

  • Built the interface and media controls so users can type and run playback entirely by hand sign.
  • Tuned detection for the letter pairs that get confused most, then led the testing that put accuracy at 97%.
  • Prototyped a Gemma integration so the tool could take commands in plain language.
